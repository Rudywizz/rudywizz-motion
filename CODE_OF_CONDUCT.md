# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, be patient with newcomers, critique work and not people, and assume
good intent. People from every country, language and skill level are welcome here;
questions in English or Turkish are both fine.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, personal attacks,
publishing others' private information, and sustained disruption.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules,
and may block people who repeatedly do so.

To report a problem privately, contact the maintainer through their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/Rudywizz). Reports are handled confidentially.
